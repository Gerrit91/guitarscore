ASCII tablature symbols used by `gscore-cli dump`

After a fret or beat
  L      tie (on the first note; the continuation is a plain fret)
  x      dead note
  (5)    ghost note: the fret in parentheses
  h      hammer-on
  p      pull-off
  b      bend up, followed by the target fret
  r      release bend, followed by the target fret
  /      slide up
  \      slide down
  S      shift slide, when the target note is unknown
  s      legato slide, when the target note is unknown
  ~      vibrato
  tr     trill
  t      tapping
  TP     tremolo picking
  *      natural harmonic
  7[19]  pinch or artificial harmonic: the node after the fret
  7(19)  tapped harmonic: written fret, then the node
  >      accent
  .      staccato
  <      fade in
  w      whammy bar
  v      brush stroke up
  ^      brush stroke down
  V      pick stroke up
  n      pick stroke down
  T      slap (thumb)
  P      pop

Rows under the staff
  PM     palm mute (its mark starts a run, `-` continues it, `|` ends it)
  LR     let ring (its mark starts a run, `-` continues it, `|` ends it)
