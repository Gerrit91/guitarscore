# Guitar Pro Feature Parity Checklist

This document tracks which Guitar Pro features are implemented in GuitarScore.
Updated 2026-11-14 (design mode: bar widths and moved beat columns; and the notation,
layout and mixer work up to Stems).

## Implemented Features

| Feature | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Read GP3, GP4, GP5, GPX (GP6) and GP7+ files | Live |
| Save GP7+ files safely (`.bak`, atomic replace, re-read check) | Live; track color, mixer volume/pan, mute and solo are kept, and anything else the format cannot keep is reported before writing |
| `info`, ASCII-tab `dump`, `roundtrip-check` | Live |
| Song information, tracks, instruments, tunings, time signatures | Live |
| Track and song grid (bottom panel) | Partial: one row per track with color, instrument, name, mute/solo, volume slider (always-visible, editable decibel readout), pan knob and a small green activity indicator; a measure matrix with note squares, measure numbers, repeats and alternative endings, section titles in the master row (clear of numbers and repeat signs), the playing measure highlighted, and a click/drag selection of measures. Add/delete/duplicate/rename/move tracks by right-click, all undoable; a header to switch to the mixer, add a track (with a name/color/symbol popup), move the shown one, mute all and solo all; a master row with mute/solo-all, volume, pan and activity; mute in red and solo in amber while engaged, with muted and solo-silenced tracks dimmed in the matrix, the track column and the mixer (2026-11-06). Clicking a track or a cell during playback switches what the page shows without stopping or seeking (only another measure seeks) |
| Mixer view (M18) | Partial: one strip per track with a logarithmic fader and gain readouts that match the synthesizer (a track tops out at 0 dB; the master is 0 dB at its default and has +4.7 dB of headroom), gain and meter scales, two-segment L/R estimated activity meter (recent note velocities, not measured audio), editable dB level, mute/solo, pan, reverb and chorus send knobs (heard at once, undoable, saved), name, instrument preset and color bar, plus a master strip with mute/solo-all and a Reverb bus (a computed reverb or a room's impulse response, chosen in its own window, every modelled track sends into it with its mixer Reverb amount; its level, mute and knobs are kept in the settings) (2026-11-06); mute in red and solo in amber while engaged, muted and solo-silenced strips dimmed (2026-11-06); toggled from the grid header and remembered with its own height ; a chorus bus (each track's Chorus value is its send; runs all wet, its own window and fader), a sound chain on the master (stored with the song, after the buses and before the fader, the click added after it), and a drum track's parts as narrower strips with their own meter, fader, mute, solo, chain and Room send, opened by the strip's arrow (2026-11-07); a kit's one short shared room behind its parts' and its own Room knobs, editable (size, decay, damping and tone) from the Sound chain window's "Kit room…", soloing a part soloing its track, an input trim before every track's chain (starting at −6 dB) with meters for its input and the chain's output, and one WAV per track and per mixer bus (File › Export › Stems) (2026-11-14) |
| Track panel (right panel, M15) | Live: name (click to edit, or the pencil), a color bar that opens a color picker (presets or any), an instrument icon that opens a picker of fifteen drawn symbols (independent of the sound; GP7 keeps it), instrument kind, the sound source (MIDI synth, PM synthesis or a hardware MIDI out, shared with the Sound chain window), the General MIDI program or drum kit, or the built-in PM tone library plus the user's saved tones, in a filterable table grouped by family (a tone is given to that track and travels in the song; a GP7 RSE sound starts on its nearest match, chosen by the sound's name, path and chain), PM synthesis by default for string tracks, drum kit, three staff switches (standard notation, tablature and slash notation, at least one on), and a tuning dialog (string visualization, sixteen common tunings or each string's open note, string count and capo), all as commands with undo; sound and tuning changes are heard while playing |
| App shell and settings (M12) | Live: docked panels with remembered sizes and visibility, a centered settings window with the app's title bar, tabs Song / Page layout / Editor / Metronome (click volume and sound, count-in length, count-in before each loop pass) / Audio I/O (sound font dropdown, MIDI ports), in-app shortcut editor stored in the settings JSON, JSON settings in the config directory, recent files, drag and drop, window position/size, zoom keys, progress and cancel for opening and SoundFont loading, cursor following playback, reopening the last file at startup (on by default, with an Editor setting; a missing file starts blank) |
| Song information editing | Live, undoable: title, subtitle, artist, album, lyricist, composer, copyright, tab author, instructions, notes and the initial tempo (settings window or `set_song_text`/`set_tempo`) |
| Guitar Pro's design mode (layout) | Live (2026-11-14): the Staff row's design tile (right after the line-break tile) shows a dashed guide and a drag handle above every beat column and barline; dragging moves the column (with everything after it and the barline) or changes the bar's width, with a yellow guide that snaps to another bar's line; `+`/`-` at the end of a line take one bar away (the rest stretch) or give one back (the next line's first bar, when a break holds it, else the bars narrow until one more fits) and `x` puts the line's layout back. A bar's width is kept per staff and overrides the song's (`set_measure_scale`); a beat's offset is kept on its beat (`set_beat_spacing`), both undoable and in the recovery journal. A GP7/GP8 file's own design data (bar width `XProperty`s) is read and written back; a moved beat has no field this code knows in GP8, so it travels in a GuitarScore property, and GP3-5 saves report it as lost |
| Edit palette (left panel, M14) | Live: note lengths (whole to 64th), dots, tuplets, rests, ties, dead notes, voice 1/2, dynamics (ppp to fff; new notes take the dynamic in effect, copy/paste keeps it), and the effect matrix; a Staff section at the top opens small dialogs for the bar's tempo (note + `=`), time signature (4/4), key signature, clef (treble, bass, alto, tenor), free time (no metronome), one- and two-bar repetition marks (icons that switch on and off), section marker, start and end repeats (separate icons), alternative endings (their own icon), swing feel (straight, triplet or dotted), fermatas, double barline, line/page break and bars per line (2026-11-05); the bend and whammy tiles (next to each other) open graph editors with draggable points, built-in presets (Bend, Bend/Release, Pre-Bend, ...; Dip, Dive, Release up, ...) and the user's own saved presets (2026-11-03) |
| Selection and clipboard (M14) | Live: a beat range in one track/voice (across bar lines and empty bars; Shift+Left/Right, dragging, Shift+Up/Down a line up or down on the page, Shift+Home/End to the track's ends; the song grid shows every selection, the score's too, over its squares in the score's selection color) or a measure range in one track or across tracks (2026-11-07); Delete clears the selected notes; copy, cut and paste as one undo step, carrying notes, durations, ties, effects, time signature, tempo and section; with only the cursor on a note, Copy/Paste move that one note (its fret and effects) to another string (2026-11-07); Copy and Cut also put a line of text on the system clipboard, without which a desktop never sends Ctrl+V on |
| Note and beat effects (read, dump, save) | Live |
| Measure issues (under/overfilled bars, notes on missing strings) | Live |

## In Progress / Partial

| Feature | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Basic tablature editing | Live: in the app at a cursor (frets with two-digit entry, clear note, shorter/longer, insert/delete beat and measure, Right fills the measure and appends measures, durations/dots/tuplets, rests, ties, dead notes, effects, voice 2) and as headless commands (`gscore-cli edit`) |
| Undo/Redo | Partial: in memory in `gscore-edit`, in the Edit menu and Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y, and in the History window (newest first, with UTC time, command and arguments; a table to undo/redo any change); not persisted across a normal restart |
| Cursor and selection | Live: cursor (keys, Home/End, page click, grid click: the bar's first beat, shift-click or drag selects bars), beat selection by Shift+arrows or dragging on the page (across bar lines and empty bars; navigation follows written order, not repeats), measure ranges in the grid for one track or all (2026-11-07) |
| Copy / cut / paste | Live for a single note, beat and measure selections (M14; single notes, one-track bars 2026-11-07) |
| Multiple voices per track | Partial: read, viewed and edited per voice; no cross-voice editing |
| Tempo changes | Partial: per measure (`set_measure_tempo`, palette) and as read from GP3-5 beats; written in the score where the tempo changes. No gradual tempo changes (accelerando, ritardando) |
| Dynamics | Live: ppp to fff per beat (`set_dynamic`, palette), played as note velocity, written under the notation (or the tab) where they change; mf, Guitar Pro's default, is not written. No hairpins (crescendo/diminuendo) |
| Drum notation | Live: drum tracks on a five-line percussion staff (percussion clef, each General MIDI drum sound in its usual place with its notehead: crosses for hi-hat and cymbals, circled crosses for open hi-hat and side stick, ...), stems up; entered as MIDI note numbers on the kit's "strings". No drum tab, no custom drum maps, no second (feet) voice |
| Time signature changes | Live: per measure from the Staff dialog and `set_time_signature`, in every track; kept and copied to new measures |
| Repeat signs, alternative endings and double barlines | Live: edited from the Staff dialogs and `set_repeat` / `set_double_bar`; drawn and played (repeats and endings); D.C./D.S./coda and fine are followed in playback (not drawn yet) |
| Feel (swing, triplet, dotted) | Live: per measure from the Staff dialog and `set_feel`; playback and `.mid` export swing eighth/sixteenth notes (two-thirds/one-third) or dotted pairs (three-quarters/one-quarter); GP7/GPX keep all five values, GP3-5 the eighth/sixteenth triplet feel |
| Fermatas | Partial: one per measure from the Staff dialog and `set_fermata`, drawn above the staff; GP7/GPX keep it, GP3-5 do not (reported by the save check); playback holds the end of the measure |
| Lyrics, chord diagrams, fingering | Live (2026-11-06): five lyric lines under the tab, edited per line in a dialog and kept in GP3-5 and GP6/GP7; chord diagrams created, edited, drawn above their beat and removed (name, grid, dots, base fret); left- and right-hand fingerings edited from the technique tiles and drawn with the notes |
| Keymap configuration | Live: a table in Settings → Editor for every action (typed characters / key names, layout independent), key capture with conflict replacement, per-row revert and reset-all, stored in the settings; `gscore-cli keymap` still lists ids |
| Fretboard, keyboard and drum views | Partial: three floating windows (toolbar buttons at the top right in a wide window, Tools menu): a fretboard with the cursor beat's notes as named circles, a piano keyboard with the beat's keys in blue, and a drum map (MIDI note, drum, kit part) with the beat's drums marked; a click enters or removes a note (2026-11-14). No left-handed fretboard, no scale display, no keyboard range choice, no custom drum maps, no hearing a note on click |
| Desktop app | Partial (`guitarscore`): new song, open file (dialog, recent files, drag and drop or command line), keyboard editing with undo/redo and the History window, entered notes heard at once, save/save as `.gp` (with the unsaved-changes question), track picker, zoom keys, light/dark theme, settings window, play/pause/stop/navigation with the built-in synthesizer or to a MIDI port, the cursor following playback, and the track and song grid with live mixing, plus the track panel (with a rename button and a tuning dialog) and a tuner with a reference tone (overtones on low notes); right-click menus on the score and Track/Bar/Note menus in the menu bar (insert/delete bars, add/duplicate/move/delete tracks, techniques with their keys); opens maximized (full screen if closed so), F11 full screen (2026-11-03) |
| Playback | Partial: built-in SoundFont synthesizer (desktop, and an AudioWorklet in the browser), MIDI output (whole-song, and per track to a hardware synthesizer with its own port and channel) and `.mid` export, and WAV export (16-bit stereo, desktop and `gscore-cli wav`), and stems (a WAV per track and per bus): all tracks with their instruments, volume, pan, reverb and chorus, tempo changes, repeats and alternative endings, D.C./D.S./coda and fine, fermatas, swing/triplet, dotted and Scottish feel, ties, dead notes, harmonics, bends, whammy and slides (on borrowed channels, so the rest of the chord keeps its pitch), vibrato, trills, tremolo picking, ghost notes, accents, staccato, palm mute, let ring, fades, brush strokes and arpeggios (each with its speed and timing), grace notes, bar repeats (played as the bars they repeat), mute and solo; a palette effect or dynamic plays its beat. Metronome with its own click (wood block, beep or cowbell; volume on its mixer fader and in Settings › Metronome; silent in free-time bars) and a count-in of 1 to 4 bars that leads straight into the song, also before each loop pass; rewind/forward one bar (during a count-in too), jump to the top/end, play from a selection and loop its bars; playback speed 25-150 % (presets or typed) with a speed trainer that raises it every few loop passes up to a typed target; a cursor that glides between the notes and a light tint on the cursor's bar; optionally back to where playback started on Stop (2026-11-11); position and total length ("00:00 / 03:45"). Not yet: Guitar Pro-like guitar sound on every passage (M22, in progress: PM synthesis is the default for string tracks with a built-in tone library and a GP7 RSE match) |
| Tablature display | Partial: standard notation above tab per track (or either alone, plus slash notation), in the app, as SVG and as PDF (`gscore-cli render`): clef changes per measure (treble, bass, alto and tenor), key signatures (at every system start and at key changes, on the notation staff or on the tab staff when notation is hidden), time signatures (parenthesized in free time, which is labelled), notes with key-aware accidentals, stems, flags, beams, dots, rests, ties (also across barlines), tuplet numbers, grace notes (cue-sized with their own accidentals and a slashed flag, smaller fret numbers on the tab, kept clear of the main note) and two or more voices per staff (stems up/down); tab fret numbers with ornaments (slurs, bends, slides, vibrato, harmonics, accents, palm mute, let ring, whammy) and tuplet brackets in their own row under the tab when no notation staff carries them (2026-11-11); section names (framed), tempo changes, repeat signs with counts, bar-repetition (simile) marks, alternative-ending brackets, fermatas and dynamics; octave signs (8va, 8vb, 15ma, 15mb) and chord diagrams; a separate slash-notation staff per track (with its ties, within a measure and across barlines); drum tracks on a percussion staff. Five views (View menu): page vertical, page horizontal, page grid, screen vertical and screen horizontal (the whole score on one line), with the song's page setup (paper, orientation, margins, score size; Settings › Page layout, read from and saved to GP7's style sheet), page numbers, copyright and an optional running header, lines spread down full pages, line and page breaks per bar and a bars-per-line limit (Bar and Track menus; GP7 line breaks kept), and PDF export with embedded fonts (File › Export › PDF, `gscore-cli render -o x.pdf`) (2026-11-03). Several tracks in one score (View › All tracks or a chosen set; `gscore-cli render --all-tracks`) with their beats lined up, GP7's multitrack line breaks kept (2026-11-05). A track's several staves (a keyboard's treble and bass) stack in their own clefs with a brace and one barline, notation only, named once, with beats lined up across the staves (2026-11-13). No D.C./D.S./coda or native printing yet |

## Testing Policy

All imported/exported files must pass round-trip tests and preserve musical content.
Every editing command is covered by apply-then-undo, `panic_hunt`, a property test, and a
save/reload check.
